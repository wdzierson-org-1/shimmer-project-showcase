# ThreeUI reference and attribution

The journey renderer adapts the Fibonacci node distribution and distance-linked topology in Meng To’s ThreeUI `nexus-topology.html`. Particle lettering and the open contour horizon also draw visual inspiration from the Particle Wordmark and Topo Field studies. The player, field morphing, glyph renderer, chapter geometry, and narrative are original to this portfolio. No ThreeUI demo pages, remote scripts, assets, or fonts are loaded at runtime.
