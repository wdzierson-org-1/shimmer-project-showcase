# At Noodle

Noodle was an edtech platform building tools for higher education — connecting students with programs, advisors with applicants, and institutions with data. I led design across the student-facing product and the institutional dashboard.

## What I built

Student discovery and enrollment flows, an advisor communication platform, and a data visualization layer for institutional clients tracking enrollment funnels. The work required balancing the needs of very different users — an anxious 22-year-old choosing a grad program and a VP of Admissions managing thousands of applicants.

## Why it mattered

Education is one of the most consequential decisions a person makes, and the digital tools around it were (and largely still are) terrible. The work here was about bringing clarity and confidence to a process that is, for most people, deeply stressful.
