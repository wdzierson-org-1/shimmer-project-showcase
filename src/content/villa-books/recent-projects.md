# Building on my own

After years of working inside institutions, I started building things for myself. Some are products. Some are experiments. All of them are attempts to figure out what's actually possible when you control the whole stack — design, engineering, product, and vision.

## Stash

A personal life organization app built around the idea of a universal inbox. Photos, voice memos, documents, links, random thoughts — all captured in one place, organized automatically with AI. Built with React Native, Supabase, and a lot of late nights.

## weOS

A concept for a next-generation operating system designed around how people actually work — nonlinear, collaborative, and context-aware. Currently an interactive prototype exploring what the desktop metaphor could look like if it were invented today.

## This site

This portfolio is itself a project. It's a hand-built, animated, interactive experience — not a template. The island you're exploring right now is a Three.js scene I designed and coded from scratch.
