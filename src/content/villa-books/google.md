# At Google

Two years embedded in Google's health and assistant teams, working at the intersection of voice interfaces, natural language, and healthcare data. The work was about making genuinely complex systems feel simple and trustworthy to the people who depended on them most.

## What I built

Worked across Google Assistant's health Q&A surface and a series of internal health data tools. Designed conversation flows, information architecture, and visual systems for features used by millions — while also writing production TypeScript and building component prototypes that engineers could actually ship.

## Why it mattered

Healthcare information is high-stakes. Getting the hierarchy of confidence, the framing of uncertainty, and the tone of clinical language right wasn't just a design problem — it was a patient safety problem. I learned to think about design as a systems problem, not just a visual one.
