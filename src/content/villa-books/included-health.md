# At Included Health

Four years as a staff designer building the navigation layer between patients and their entire healthcare system — benefits, care navigation, virtual visits, second opinions. The product had to work for a 27-year-old with a sprained ankle and a 58-year-old managing three chronic conditions.

## What I built

Led design on the multimodal AI assistant — a voice, chat, and video-enabled care navigation interface that could triage, route, and engage patients across their care journey. Also built the design system and core navigation patterns that unified the product across web and native.

## Why it mattered

Most healthcare apps fail because they optimize for the institution, not the patient. This work was about inverting that — putting a coherent, trustworthy experience in front of people at the moments they needed the system most.
