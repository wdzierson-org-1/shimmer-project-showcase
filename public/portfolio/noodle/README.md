# Noodle homepage assets

Original artwork and recordings from Will Dzierson's Noodle site, https://www.noodleai.app/, retrieved September 9, 2026 for the portfolio homepage reconstruction.

- `logo.svg`: `/_next/static/media/logo_noodle.830505f4.svg`.
- `doodle{1,2,3,4,6,8,15}.svg`: `/images/doodles/svg/doodle{number}.svg`, preserved as vectors.
- `icon{1..6}.png`: `/images/icons/icon{number}.png` (displayed at the original 40px size).
- `inter-latin.woff2`: `/_next/static/media/e4af272ccee01ff0-s.p.woff2`. Inter is distributed under the included `INTER-LICENSE.txt`, obtained from https://github.com/rsms/inter/blob/master/LICENSE.txt.
- `walkthrough-poster.webp`: encoded from `/_next/static/media/hero-video-sc.0f02f9b1.png`, retaining 2896×1589 dimensions.
- `voice-poster.webp`, `messages-poster.webp`, `recall-poster.webp`: frames at 0.5s from `/videos/noodle_meds_vid_1.mp4`, `/videos/noodle_web_mobile_1.mp4`, and `/videos/noodle_mobile_2.mp4`, respectively, scaled to 600px wide.

Video playback streams the original recordings on demand from noodleai.app. The layout, typography, original artwork, and posters are local; no remote scripts or stylesheets are embedded. The page is presented as historical work and collects no visitor information.
